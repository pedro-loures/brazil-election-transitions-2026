Estimated transitions, with uncertainty visible

These are retrospective expected flows inferred from aggregate election returns. They are not surveyed voting intentions, individual ballot histories, or evidence of campaign causation.

Map: verified municipality polygons. Gray areas were outside the pilot. Polling-place estimates are in the detail selector; physical coordinates and catchment boundaries were not verified.

Municipal agreement and investigation

All comparisons use identical retained sections. The baseline fixes expected municipal destination totals; the hierarchical model predicts them. Agreement of observed totals is a data check, not hidden-flow validation. Interval overlap is not an agreement test.

Known-flow validation

Expected-flow truth is known in simulation only. Coverage below is an average over correlated flow cells, with Monte Carlo error calculated across independent repetitions. It is not measured local accuracy for any municipality. Baseline intervals are bootstrap sensitivity ranges; new intervals are approximate-model posterior credible intervals.

Longer-chain computational confirmation

Post-screen refits of three existing datasets per scenario, with four chains and 1,500 draws per chain. They do not add independent repetitions or replace the original screen.

Data and definitions

The intervals are pointwise and conditional on grouping, priors, population closure, and a multivariate normal approximation to a multinomial convolution. Structural zeros, weak designs, and contextual bias can cause failure. No composite reliability score is used. Prior sensitivity is the largest point-estimate shift across two predeclared prior variants; it is unavailable for the original baseline.
