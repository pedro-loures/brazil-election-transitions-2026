What these flows establish

The thread reference reproduces the estimated flows shown in your screenshots. Municipality views use our explicit common-transition model fitted to matched, validated sections. Individual votes and voter movements are not observed.

The full model permits switching between finalists. The comparison model prohibits it. Model flows reconcile to the analyzed municipality margins by construction; individual section fits can have residuals. Bootstrap ranges show section-resampling sensitivity under the model.

Different source snapshots and excluded sections can change the totals. The thread uses all 30 sections of Lebon Régis; our current matched-electorate analysis retains 29. Machine-log timing has not been analyzed.

State, region, and Brazil diagrams sum the available municipal model matrices; they are not new geographic regressions. Their coverage excludes municipalities without a fitted matrix and sections omitted from those fits. Full observed geographic totals include every municipality in that area. Aggregate bootstrap intervals have not been computed; municipal interval endpoints are not added together. Brazil excludes overseas voting.
